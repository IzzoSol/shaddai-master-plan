01The economy in one page

SHADDAI runs on sparks, an in-app unit at a $1 peg, divisible to 6 decimals — 0.5 sparks is 50 cents. Sparks settle play (tickets, plots, quests). Real value (jobs, packs, tiers) settles in USDC over the x402 rail. A token is optional and undecided; there is no staking.

The peg holds when sinks ≥ faucets: capped plot yield (72/day, max 3, 24h bank), ticket / plot / hire sinks, market-commission sinks and a death-penalty sink — plus USDC buy pressure.

Every unit of revenue is allocated by an internal split 30 / 20 / 20 / 15 / 10 / 5: reserve, treasury ops, founders + community share, sinks, farms + trees, giveaways. subject to change

04Token — optional, no staking

Status: undecided. SHADDAI works fully without a token. Sparks are an in-app unit at a $1 peg and are not a tradable asset. A token is an optional future add-on, only if you decide to use one.

No token staking. No staking, no lockups, no APR, no yield promises, in any scenario.

If a token is ever added: it needs human legal review first (issuance, custody, regional rules), its own ledger account kept separate from sparks and compute credits, and a decision on supply and utility before any build. Nothing in this hub depends on it.

05Founders passes, farms, siege

Founders passes (capped): Genesis 100 (plot + 25% fee discount + revenue-share perk + lobby-box) · Pioneer 500 (10% off + drops + half-off tickets) · Settler 1000 (title + cosmetic + XP). Issued after first real revenue. subject to change

Farms: plant fee splits reserve 50 / replant 40 / farm owner 10. A farm pass gives half-off colosseum tickets at the gate.

Castle siege: server ticket 25 sparks, 5 dragons, damage weights 40 / resets 20 / max-hit 20 / final blow 10 / council 10. Rewards: USDC + sparks + siege cosmetics + titles.

Marketplace: feeds, item swaps, clothing, agent bodies, cosmetics from 0.1 sparks — bazaar fees on all of it.

06Compliance + honesty ledger
Paper account $10k / 0 trades — no verified edge claimed. Wallets unfunded, paywall off — everything above is paper until gas + first USDC. No yield or return promises anywhere.

Rules that never change: quote_id only (never amounts) · idempotent buys · PIKADON veto before payouts · caps $20/tx, $100/day · secrets never in logs · losses published with wins.
