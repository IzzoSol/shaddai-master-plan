01Whitepaper — the economy in one page

SHADDAI runs two monies. Sparks settle play (tickets, plots, quests) at a $1 peg, divisible to 6 decimals like sats — 0.5 sparks is 50 cents. USDC + $SHADDAI settle real value (jobs, packs, tiers) over the x402 rail.

Every unit of revenue splits 30 / 20 / 20 / 15 / 10 / 5: liquidity pools, treasury, holder + founder dividends, burns + locks, farms + trees, giveaways. Holding sparks earns a SOL share of the holders slice — dividends, never promised yield. Staking is deferred until revenue is real; founders NFTs are the pre-staking.

The peg holds iff sinks ≥ faucets: capped plot yield (72/day, max 3, 24h bank), ticket/plot/hire sinks, market-commission burns, death-penalty sink — plus USDC buy pressure. Token launches backed by treasury + real hall-cut revenue, never promises.

04Token + staking status

$SHADDAI pairs SHADDAI/SOL, fixed supply, LP locked, mint renounced at launch. Utility: fee discounts, priority routing, arena perks, fee-param governance.

Staking: DEFERRED. Replaced by founders NFTs + holding dividends until hall-cut revenue is real. No lockups, no APR — the staking loop workflow (staking-stake-claim-loop) stays published but gated.

$ASIMOV (separate idea): launch on stonksx.fun with NVDAx reserve, ASIMOV center in-game, shard-hunt CA reveal, flywheel NVDAx → SHADDAI → SOL → Sparks. NVDAx is geo-restricted (excl. US) — jurisdiction plan required, no workarounds.

05Founders, farms, siege

Founders (capped): Genesis 100 (plot + 25% fees off + dividend + lobby-box) · Pioneer 500 (10% off + airdrops + half-off tickets) · Settler 1000 (title + cosmetic + XP). Mint after first real revenue.

Farms: plant → 50% LP, 40% replant, 10% farm owner. Owners earn off others' planting + harvest cosmetics; planters earn yield + XP. Farm NFT = half-off colosseum tickets at the gate.

Castle siege: server ticket 25 sparks, 5 dragons, pool = sales −15% hall + sponsors. Weights: damage 40 / resets 20 / max-hit 20 / final blow 10 / council 10. Payouts: USDC + sparks + siege cosmetics + titles. Anti-cheat rails apply.

Marketplace: feeds, item swaps, clothing, agent bodies, cosmetics from 0.1 sparks — bazaar fees on all of it.
